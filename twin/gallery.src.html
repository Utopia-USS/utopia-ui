        14
        
Principles

        
      

      

        The rules the components hold themselves to.
      

      

        
1
No hardcoded values
Components never inline a color, gap, radius or duration. Everything reads the ambient theme, which is why one knob repaints this whole page.

        
2
One base unit
Spacing and radii are multiples of x. Off-scale values (a 10px gap, a 14px inset) are bugs, not fine-tuning.

        
3
One accent family
Primary and accent are a tight same-hue pair - the button gradient, chips and selection states all draw from it, so an app never fights itself for attention.

        
4
Semantic slots over raw tokens
A component asks for cardRadius or fieldContentPadding, not "16". The decision of which token a role sits on lives in the theme, in one place.

        
5
Controlled components
Tables, fields and dialogs render state and report interaction - they never own app state. Your models, your data layer, your navigation.

        
6
Slivers compose
The table is a sliver, not a widget with its own scroll view - it shares the page scroll with everything around it.
